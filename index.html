Projects

LLM & ML Evaluation of NYS ELA Reading Passages
Spring 2026 — with Howell Lu & Ruiting Shen

Evaluated whether grade-level complexity of 2024–2025 New York State ELA reading passages is recoverable by LLMs and traditional readability metrics. Deployed Claude Opus 4.5 alongside six local models via Ollama and a Random Forest on Flesch-Kincaid, Dale-Chall, and CTTR features. All models showed systematic grade-level compression; the Random Forest achieved the lowest RMSE.

Psychometric Modeling of Duolingo Spanish Reverse-Tap Exercises
Spring 2026 — with Shen Shen Lau

Applied EFA, Rasch IRT, and Cognitive Diagnostic Models to ~540K Duolingo trial responses. Near-ceiling accuracy (~95%) precluded latent class recovery; a pivot to response-time-based CDM yielded non-degenerate results. Verb tense/mood emerged as the most consistent driver of item difficulty across all three frameworks.

Social Connectedness and Central American Migration to the U.S.
Spring 2026 — with Genie Kwak

Analyzed Meta's Social Connectedness Index alongside DHS lawful permanent resident data (2013–2024). Four Central American countries exceeded global SCI averages by wide margins — Honduras at ~5× — and the SCI–immigration relationship was substantially stronger for Central America than the global baseline.
