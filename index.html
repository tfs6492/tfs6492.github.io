Projects

  • Psychometric Modeling of Duolingo Spanish Reverse-Tap Exercises (2026, with Shen Shen Lau)
    Applied EFA, Rasch IRT, and Cognitive Diagnostic Modeling to ~540K exercise trials to predict item difficulty from morphosyntactic features. Pivoted to response-time-based fluency modeling after a ceiling effect (~94.7% accuracy) rendered accuracy-based approaches degenerate.
  • Are New York State ELA Test Reading Passages Robust to LLM Scrutiny? (2026, with Howell Lu & Ruiting Shen)
    Compared Claude Opus 4.5, three local models via Ollama, and a Random Forest on Flesch-Kincaid/Dale-Chall/CTTR features to evaluate grade-level reading complexity in the NYST ELA. All models showed systematic compression toward the middle of the grade scale.
  • Borders Crossed, Connections Kept: Central American Migration and the Social Connectedness Index (2026, with Genie Kwak)
    Used Meta's Social Connectedness Index to show that Central American nations have anomalously dense social ties with the U.S., arguing the SCI serves as a viable proxy for migration footprint even when undocumented populations are excluded.
